# 2026-10-11

## Fix: a Phantom from another account paid for the signed-in investor

Reported by a teammate: after signing out and signing in with a new email, a subscription went through from Phantom, and the tokens landed with the previous account. Three causes together:

- Signing out left the wallet adapter connected (autoconnect), so the next account on the browser got the previous account's Phantom. Sign out now disconnects it.
- The investor app always let a connected Phantom sign. It now signs only when it is linked to the signed-in account; otherwise the email wallet signs and the panel says why.
- The subscribe gate (`kfdGate` in `/api/tx/subscribe` and the sponsor check in `/api/tx/send`) checked the wallet's owner and never read the session. With someone signed in, the wallet must now be linked to that account (`WalletOtherAccount`, 403). Unauthenticated wallet-only subscriptions on the public pool page keep the old check.

Tests: `test/workflow-investor.test.ts`, `test/routes/subscribe-gate.test.ts`, `test/routes/send.test.ts`, `test/cabinet-invest.test.tsx`.
