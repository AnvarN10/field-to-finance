# 2026-10-07

## Cabinets redesign: separate doors and one permission-based console

User decision (2026-10-07): the post-login "I am a farmer / I am an investor" picker and the six role cabinets go. Investors self-register, farmers apply, service parties are invited into one back office whose sections and buttons follow permissions. Recorded in [ADR 0018](../architecture/decisions/0018-separate-doors-and-permission-based-console.md); design in the [spec](../specs/2026-10-07-cabinets-redesign.md); [plan](../plans/2026-10-07-cabinets-redesign.md).

- Research (Russian, 33 screenshots, PDF): [report-ru.md](../research/cabinets-ux/report-ru.md). RWA platforms (Securitize, Tokeny, Ondo, Centrifuge, Maple, Goldfinch, Superstate) and crowdlending/agri-finance (Mintos, Bondora, iKapitalist, FarmTogether, AcreTrader, Harvest Returns, PeerBerry, Funding Circle): pattern catalogue, what to borrow, proposed IA and permission matrix.
- Doors: `/invest/signup` (investor account opens right after email sign-in), `/apply` + `/apply/start` (farmer explainer and issuer workspace), `/invite/<token>` (console). `/sign-in` sends each account home; an email without an account sees the two doors.
- Apps: investor app `/invest` (Overview, Opportunities, Portfolio, Activity, Account, pool page with the KFD gate) and issuer workspace `/issuer` (Overview with one next step in the farmer's words, Applications, Obligations, Company), each with its own top bar.
- Console `/console`: left navigation filtered by permission, Inbox of work queues, Applications, Issuers, Investors, Pools, Settlement, Verification, Delivery, Registry, Audit, Team (invitations, members, permission matrix), Settings.
- Permissions: 18 permissions, console roles as bundles in code (`src/lib/permissions.ts`), checked in pages, actions, routes and workflows. Migration `0003_console_invitations` (token hash only, single use, 7 days, email-bound). No data backfill: existing `user_roles` keep working.
- Old URLs redirect (temporary redirects from one table).
- Screenshots after: [screens/f2f-after/](../research/cabinets-ux/screens/f2f-after/); before: the 2026-10-05 cabinet screenshots in [2026-10-05-screens/](2026-10-05-screens/).
- Not deployed: production deploy waits for the user's confirmation.
